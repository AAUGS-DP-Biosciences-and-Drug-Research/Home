Group Name: Cell Migration Lab

Subject: Cell Biology

University: Åbo Akademi University

Lab Website: link

AboCRIS Profile: link

Web Pages: ORCID · Google Scholar

Areas of Expertise

  • Cancer cell migration, invasion, and metastasis
  • Filopodia, cell adhesion, and cytoskeletal dynamics
  • Endothelial barriers and cancer-endothelium interactions
  • Advanced microscopy and quantitative live-cell imaging
  • Bioimage analysis, cell tracking, deep learning, and computer vision

Research Projects

  • Mechanisms of pancreatic cancer arrest and extravasation at vascular barriers
  • Filopodia in cancer invasion and cell-cell communication
  • Endothelial barrier regulation and immune-endothelial interactions
  • FlowVision: label-free imaging and quantitative analysis of cell arrest under flow
  • Accessible deep learning and reproducible bioimage analysis for microscopy
  • Tracking, registration, and quantitative microscopy tools

Special Methodologies & Techniques

  • Live-cell, spinning-disk, and super-resolution microscopy
  • Microfluidic flow, perfusion, and endothelial barrier assays
  • Quantitative image analysis, cell tracking, segmentation, and computer vision
  • Deep learning for microscopy and reproducible bioimage-analysis workflows
  • Cell and molecular biology and protein-interaction analysis
  • Zebrafish and mouse models of cancer progression and metastasis
